A government department is planning to implement a digital service delivery system to improve public access to government services. The proposed project aims to serve 100,000 citizens annually and reduce service processing times by 40% within three years. The department is considering solutions that can integrate with existing government systems and improve coordination between public institutions. The department wants to understand implementation costs, approval requirements, data protection measures, and staff training needs. They are also concerned about cybersecurity, system reliability, public accessibility, and long-term maintenance.
