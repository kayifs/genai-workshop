Conduct a comprehensive deep-research study to develop a high-impact international marketing campaign for the Board of Investment of Sri Lanka (BOI) to promote its Flagship Investment Projects listed on:

https://readytoinvestsl.com/

OBJECTIVE

Develop a data-driven marketing strategy that can attract qualified international investors and strategic partners to Sri Lanka’s flagship investment projects, generate credible investor interest, and convert that interest into enquiries, EOIs, and investment discussions.

RESEARCH SCOPE

1. FLAGSHIP PROJECT LANDSCAPE
- Review the Ready to Invest Sri Lanka website in detail.
- Identify all projects classified as “Flagship Projects”.
- Group them by sector, investment size, location, project maturity, target investor type, and investment proposition.
- Identify the strongest and most marketable projects.
- Identify projects that require more education, proof points, or investor confidence-building.

2. INVESTOR TARGETING
Identify the most relevant international investor audiences for each flagship project, including:
- Strategic investors
- Institutional investors
- Private equity and infrastructure funds
- Sovereign wealth funds
- Family offices
- Multinational corporations
- Developers and operators
- Joint-venture partners

For each audience, identify:
- Priority countries/regions
- Investment interests
- Typical investment motivations
- Key decision factors
- Concerns or barriers
- Preferred channels and information sources

3. MARKET AND COMPETITOR ANALYSIS
Research how competing investment destinations in South and Southeast Asia market large-scale investment opportunities.

Compare Sri Lanka with relevant destinations such as:
- India
- Vietnam
- Indonesia
- Malaysia
- Thailand
- Bangladesh
- UAE

Analyse their investment-promotion campaigns, messaging, digital presence, investor propositions, flagship-project marketing, events, content strategies, and calls to action.

Identify what Sri Lanka can learn, differentiate, or improve.

4. SRI LANKA INVESTMENT VALUE PROPOSITION
Research credible evidence supporting Sri Lanka’s investment proposition, including:
- Geographic position
- Access to regional and global markets
- Trade agreements and market access
- Workforce and skills
- Infrastructure
- Tourism potential
- Manufacturing and export capabilities
- Renewable energy potential
- Logistics and maritime advantages
- Government/BOI facilitation
- Relevant incentives and policy support

Also identify investor concerns and risks that must be addressed honestly rather than ignored.

5. CAMPAIGN POSITIONING
Develop 3–5 possible campaign concepts for BOI Sri Lanka.

For each concept provide:
- Campaign name
- Core idea
- Investor value proposition
- Key message
- Supporting proof points
- Emotional/strategic appeal
- Target investor segment
- Example tagline
- Example digital advertisement
- Example LinkedIn post
- Example investor email subject line

The concepts should be premium, credible, internationally relevant, and appropriate for a government investment-promotion organisation.

6. FLAGSHIP PROJECT MARKETING STRATEGY
Recommend how BOI should market the projects as a portfolio rather than as isolated projects.

Develop:
- Portfolio-level positioning
- Sector-level campaigns
- Project-level campaigns
- Investor-segment campaigns
- Country/region-specific campaigns

Show how the campaigns can work together.

7. DIGITAL MARKETING
Recommend a digital strategy covering:
- LinkedIn
- Google Search
- Display advertising
- YouTube/video
- Email marketing
- Investor landing pages
- SEO
- Digital PR
- Webinars and virtual investor briefings

Specify the role of each channel and which investor audiences it should target.

8. CONTENT STRATEGY
Recommend the content BOI should create, such as:
- Investor success stories
- Project opportunity briefs
- Investment case studies
- Sector reports
- Executive videos
- Investor testimonials
- Project walkthroughs
- Infographics
- Market opportunity reports
- FAQ content
- Due-diligence information packs

Prioritise content based on likely investor impact.

9. INTERNATIONAL INVESTOR OUTREACH
Recommend a targeted outreach programme using:
- Investment conferences
- Roadshows
- Embassies and diplomatic missions
- Chambers of commerce
- Industry associations
- Investment banks
- Private equity networks
- Sovereign wealth funds
- Family offices
- Sector-specific events

Identify the most relevant international markets and events where appropriate.

10. CAMPAIGN FUNNEL
Design an investor acquisition funnel:

Awareness → Interest → Project Exploration → Enquiry → Qualified Lead → EOI → Investor Engagement → Negotiation → Investment

For each stage identify:
- Investor objective
- BOI objective
- Content
- Channel
- Call to action
- KPI

11. DATA AND PERFORMANCE
Recommend measurable KPIs such as:
- Qualified investor leads
- Cost per qualified lead
- Website engagement
- Project page views
- Investment enquiry rate
- EOI submissions
- Investor meeting requests
- Conversion rate
- Pipeline value
- Country/sector performance

12. RECOMMENDED CAMPAIGN
Based on all research, propose one preferred campaign strategy.

Provide:
- Campaign positioning
- Target audiences
- Priority markets
- Campaign concept
- Core message
- Campaign pillars
- Channel strategy
- Content plan
- 90-day launch plan
- 6-month expansion plan
- Indicative KPI framework

RESEARCH QUALITY REQUIREMENTS

- Use current and credible sources.
- Prioritise official government, BOI, investment-promotion agencies, multilateral institutions, reputable research firms, industry bodies, and high-quality financial/business publications.
- Verify important statistics using multiple credible sources where possible.
- Clearly distinguish verified facts from analysis, assumptions, and recommendations.
- Do not invent investor data, project information, investment returns, incentives, or market statistics.
- Treat claims on the Ready to Invest Sri Lanka website as project information that should be cross-checked where appropriate.
- Highlight information that appears outdated, inconsistent, unsupported, or requires confirmation.

OUTPUT

Produce a structured strategic research report with:

1. Executive Summary
2. Flagship Project Portfolio Analysis
3. Investor Segmentation
4. Priority Geographic Markets
5. Competitor Benchmark
6. Sri Lanka Investment Positioning
7. Campaign Concepts
8. Recommended Campaign
9. Digital & Content Strategy
10. Investor Outreach Strategy
11. Investor Funnel
12. KPI Framework
13. 90-Day Action Plan
14. 6-Month Roadmap
15. Key Risks and Mitigations
16. Sources and References

For every major recommendation, explain the evidence or reasoning behind it.

The final output should be suitable for discussion by senior BOI management and marketing/investment-promotion teams.
